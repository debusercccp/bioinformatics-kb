# Introduction to Modeling Biological Data

**Revised and expanded course notes.** Pier Luigi Martelli, *Elements of Computational Biology*, University of Bologna, A.Y. 2026/27.

## How this revision is organised

Each module keeps the original structure and content, with three kinds of addition:

- **“How the operations are performed”** — for every object introduced, an explicit computational recipe: what goes in, what comes out, what the conformability condition is, and a worked numerical example.
- **“What is actually different”** — the conceptual distinctions that are routinely conflated (norm vs distance vs inner product; covariance vs correlation; orthogonal vs independent; likelihood vs probability; PCA vs clustering), set out side by side.
- **Missing material** — results used later in the course but never stated: Cauchy–Schwarz, rank–nullity, Rouché–Capelli, SVD, positive-definiteness tests, multiple-testing correction.

Two reference tables close the document:

- **Appendix A** — complete, verified solutions to every slide exercise. Several solutions circulating in the earlier draft were computed from mis-transcribed matrices; those are flagged and corrected.
- **Appendix B** — a master list of errors and typos in the slides and in the earlier draft, with the corrected statement.

Everything numerical in this document has been checked by direct computation.
